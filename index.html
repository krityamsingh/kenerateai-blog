Adult-only image generation

18+ AI Image Generator Free

The page describes an adult-only image generator for mature artwork, glamour, character art and 18+ illustration workflows.

  • Public page advertises free-to-start access and watermark-free output.
  • Advertises image output up to 4K.
  • Page lists FLUX Uncensored, Wan 2.7, Qwen 2.0, Chroma and Seedance Spicy among model options.
  • Public performance claim: roughly 2–4 second image generation.
  • Includes an image-to-video path for animating generated images.

Adult-only image editing

18+ AI Image Editor

The editor page describes upload-based image transformation with outfit modification, background changes, style transfer and artistic redesign.

  • Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP and HEIC uploads according to the public page.
  • Advertised upload size: up to 20 MB.
  • Highlights inpainting, outpainting, style transfer and full-image reimagining.
  • Advertises 4K output and approximately 3–6 second edits.
  • Example model labels include Inpainting v2, Flux Inpaint and Wan Editor.

https://kenerateai.com/18-ai-image-editor

Wan 2.7 Spicy · SEO guide

Wan 2.7 Spicy AI Video Generator

This page presents Wan 2.7 Spicy as an image-to-video workflow built around the Wan 2.7 architecture, with an emphasis on facial consistency, motion and camera control.

  • Image-to-video generation with 720p and 1080p options.
  • Public page lists 2–15 second video durations.
  • Motion prompting and cinematic camera movement controls.
  • First/last-frame morphing and identity consistency are listed as supported.
  • Advertised watermark-free MP4 export.

https://kenerateai.com/wan-2-7-spicy

Wan 2.7 Spicy · playground

Wan 2.7 Spicy Playground

The alternate URL is structured more like a focused generation playground for uploading a starting image and writing a motion prompt.

  • Starting image is required for the playground workflow.
  • Resolution controls shown for 720p and 1080p.
  • Duration controls shown for 5, 10 and 15 seconds.
  • Public copy highlights dolly, orbit, crane and push/pull-style camera motion.
  • Emphasizes character consistency and watermark-free export.
